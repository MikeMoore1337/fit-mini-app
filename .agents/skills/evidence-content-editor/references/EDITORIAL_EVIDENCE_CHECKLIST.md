# Editorial evidence checklist

## 1. Material record

```text
content id/slug
content type: article/exercise/news/digest
status/revision
locale
target audience
question/intent
author/editor/reviewer
published/updated/reviewed dates
source list
claim-source matrix
risk flags
related product contexts
indexability
```

## 2. Source verification

For every source:

- canonical publisher/URL;
- title/authors/organization;
- source type;
- publication date;
- event/study date where relevant;
- retraction/correction/update status;
- primary source available?;
- paywall/licensing constraints;
- funding/conflicts;
- relevance to claim.

Prefer primary source when multiple outlets cover the same study/announcement.

## 3. Study/report extraction

When relevant, extract without embellishment:

- design;
- population/sample;
- intervention/exposure;
- comparator;
- duration;
- primary outcomes;
- effect direction/magnitude only if verified;
- uncertainty;
- adherence/dropout;
- adverse events;
- limitations;
- generalizability.

Do not force all fields into a short post. Use them to constrain wording.

## 4. Wording strength

### Stronger wording may be justified when

- evidence is consistent and directly relevant;
- source is high quality;
- effect and context are clear;
- major limitations are disclosed.

### Qualified wording is required when

- observational;
- small/short;
- animal/in-vitro;
- surrogate outcome;
- subgroup/post-hoc;
- conflicting evidence;
- press release only;
- manufacturer-funded or seller source;
- applicability differs from target audience.

Examples of qualified language:

- `связано с` instead of `вызывает`;
- `может помочь` instead of `гарантирует`;
- `в этом исследовании` instead of `доказано навсегда`;
- `данные ограничены`;
- `результат нельзя напрямую переносить на...`.

## 5. Article gate

- direct answer is present;
- terminology explained;
- practical advice follows evidence;
- limitations visible, not buried;
- no diagnosis/treatment;
- source/reviewer dates;
- related links useful;
- no duplicate thin article;
- public metadata matches visible content;
- draft/review noindex;
- mobile reading/a11y.

## 6. Exercise page gate

- canonical exercise metadata;
- technique source;
- primary/secondary muscles without fake coefficients;
- equipment;
- alternatives curated;
- safety notes non-medical;
- media license/source;
- custom/private exercise excluded;
- missing facts not generated;
- no duplicate SEO prose.

## 7. Telegram news gate

- source allowed/trusted;
- duplicate cluster resolved;
- primary source preferred;
- Russian draft;
- what/why/application/limitations/source;
- length fits planned Telegram strategy;
- no unsupported sample/dose/effect;
- source content injection ignored;
- evidence/risk notes visible to moderator;
- exact immutable revision;
- optional image safe/original/licensed;
- no automatic publication;
- no daily quota pressure.

## 8. Digest gate

- only approved/published items;
- up to product-defined maximum;
- quality threshold may yield fewer items;
- topic diversity;
- no repeated study cluster;
- concise takeaway accurate;
- links point to published originals;
- immutable owner-approved digest;
- personal delivery has separate opt-in.

## 9. Sports nutrition gate

- product/category not disguised advertising;
- general purpose and alternatives from food;
- evidence context;
- dose/timing only if supported;
- risks/sleep/hydration/interactions where relevant;
- manufacturer source not sole evidence;
- no guaranteed performance/body outcome;
- no pharmacology/AAS/SARMs;
- no unsupported brand ranking.

## 10. Copyright/media gate

- no copied full article/large passage;
- quotation minimal and attributed;
- no paywall bypass;
- source image rights confirmed or not used;
- generated visual not presented as data;
- no fake chart/numbers;
- no public-figure likeness/trademark/packaging without rights;
- canonical logo from repository;
- alt text and mobile crop.

## 11. AI generation audit

Record:

- provider/model;
- prompt version;
- source ids/hashes;
- generated revision id;
- safety/evidence flags;
- moderator/editor;
- approval action/time;
- final content/media hash.

Tests:

- injected source instruction;
- conflicting sources;
- weak press release;
- animal/small study;
- supplement seller;
- provider unavailable;
- regenerate after approval;
- stale callback;
- fallback template;
- no unsupported claims.

## 12. Correction policy

Trigger review when:

- source correction/retraction;
- material factual error;
- misleading headline;
- changed official guidance;
- outdated product behavior;
- broken source/link;
- material translation divergence.

Record correction/replacement visibly according to product policy. Do not silently rewrite a materially wrong published post.

## 13. Final editorial gate

- every material claim traceable;
- wording strength matches evidence;
- practical section justified;
- limitations preserved;
- conflicts disclosed where material;
- legal/medical/product scope respected;
- copyright/media safe;
- status/revision/indexing correct;
- actual reviewer identified;
- no claim of review that did not occur.
