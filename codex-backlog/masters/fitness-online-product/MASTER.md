# Fitness Online-inspired product expansion master

Objective: extend Your Fitness Coach, not clone Fitness Online.

Observed baseline: programs/templates/builder/schedule/today/workout/history exist; sets already hold reps/weight/completion; `Exercise.primary_muscle` and guide technique/breathing/mistakes/images/secondary-muscle profiles exist; analytics already has adherence/streak/weight/weekly volume/PR/timeline; trainer/client relationships exist.

Observed gaps: no normalized muscle model for analytics, no RIR field, no deterministic program-selection engine/wizard, no contextual trainer comment model found, no dedicated knowledge routes found, guide UI does not surface all muscle metadata, media strategy needs explicit Web/mobile/TMA plan.

New tasks: 20-25, 33-34, 37-38.

Excluded: social network/feed, friends/followers, ratings, trainer marketplace, generic messenger, video calls, sports pharmacology.

AI boundary: no AI program selection and no Trainer Copilot.
