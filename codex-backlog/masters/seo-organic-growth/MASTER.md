# SEO + Organic Growth master brief

## Why this block is early

SEO is integrated before major product/redesign work so public routing, indexability,
content hierarchy, measurement and performance are not retrofitted at the end.

Execution order:

```text
00 technical audit
01 UI audit
02 SEO/growth audit
03 technical SEO
04 webmaster/monitoring
05 design system
06 public IA/content
07 organic promotion
08+ core product
...
48 final landing
50 performance/CWV
51 final SEO regression
```
